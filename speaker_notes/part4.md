### 🚀 Zooming Out:

* **"Let's zoom out and talk about the bigger picture."**

* **"Let me give you a clear framework."**

---

### 🌐 Three Eras of the Internet (Reference Slide Cards):
* **"Web1 — the read-only web (90s to early 2000s):"**
  * *"Websites were static. You could read information, but not interact."*
  * *"It was a static directory. Like a library."*
* **"Web2 — the read-write web (Our current era):"**
  * *"Social media, YouTube, interactive platforms."*
  * *"You create content, connect, and interact."*
  * *"But the catch: the platforms own everything — your data, your content, your identity."*
  * *"You are the product."*
* **"Web3 — the read-write-own web (The future vision):"**
  * *"Users actually own their digital lives."*
  * *"Your data isn't stored on a company's server — it's controlled by your wallet."*
  * *"Your digital assets belong to you, not a platform."*

---

### 🔑 The Ownership Economy:
* **"This idea is called the ownership economy."**
* **"In Web2: you create content on YouTube, YouTube sells ads and keeps most of the money."**
* **"In Web2: you build an Instagram following, Instagram can ban you tomorrow and they're gone."**
* **"In Web3: your wallet is your identity. Your assets move with you."**
* **"No platform can take them away. If an app shuts down, your stuff still lives on-chain."**
* **"Your wallet becomes a universal passport for the internet..."**
* **"Your login, your ownership record, your identity, all in one."**

---

### ⚖️ The Honest Counterpoint (Critical Balance):
* **"I want to be balanced here, because intellectual honesty matters."**
* **"The Web3 vision is compelling. But the reality today is messier."**
* **"Most people still access blockchain through centralized front-ends."**
* **"They buy on Coinbase. They trade on OpenSea. They manage wallets via MetaMask."**
* **"The underlying infrastructure may be decentralized..."**
* **"But the user experience layer is very much controlled by companies, just like Web2."**
* **"True decentralization remains a vision more than a reality."**
* **"That doesn't make the vision wrong. It just means we're still early."**
