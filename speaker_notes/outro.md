## ❓ Q&A REFERENCE PANEL
*(Keep this sheet in hand or on the table in front of you during questions!)*

### 1. "Is crypto a scam?"
* **Suggested Angle:** Separating speculation from tech.
* **Talking Points:** 
  * *"Some projects absolutely are scams or frauds, collapsed at scale."*
  * *"But the tech itself is not a scam, just like email spam doesn't make the internet a scam."*
  * *"Bitcoin has run for 15+ years; Ethereum processes billions daily. Separate speculation from the foundational tech."*

### 2. "Is it bad for the environment?"
* **Suggested Angle:** Depends entirely on the consensus mechanism.
* **Talking Points:**
  * *"Bitcoin's Proof of Work uses massive electricity. That's a highly valid criticism."*
  * *"But Ethereum switched to Proof of Stake in 2022 and cut energy use by ~99.95%."*
  * *"Most newer blockchains use energy-efficient Proof of Stake. The landscape is changing rapidly."*

### 3. "Should I invest?"
* **Suggested Angle:** Clear neutral line, education first.
* **Talking Points:**
  * *"That is a personal financial decision. I don't give investment advice."*
  * *"My rule: learn first, invest later. Never risk anything you can't afford to lose."*

### 4. "Will it replace banks?"
* **Suggested Angle:** Convergence, not replacement.
* **Talking Points:**
  * *"Unlikely to replace banks completely, but pressures them significantly to modernize."*
  * *"We already see banks experimenting with settlement and asset tokenization."*
  * *"Traditional finance and DeFi are converging, rather than one erasing the other."*

### 5. "What's the biggest challenge?"
* **Suggested Angle:** Human and UX bottlenecks.
* **Talking Points:**
  * *"First: User Experience. It is still way too hard and risky for regular non-technical people to use securely."*
  * *"Second: Regulation. Governments are writing the rules in real-time, which will heavily shape the future."*
