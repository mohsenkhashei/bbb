### ❓ The Core Question:
> **"If there's no central authority — no bank, no government, no server..."**
> 
> **"How does a network of thousands of strangers agree on what the truth is?"**

* **"The answer is something called a consensus mechanism."**
* **"It's the ruleset that every participant follows to agree on the same version of reality."**

---

### 🟠 Proof of Work (Orange Slide Card):
* **"Proof of Work — this is Bitcoin's approach."**
* **"Imagine a global competition happening every 10 minutes."**
* **"Thousands of computers are racing to solve a complex math puzzle."**
* **"The first to solve it adds the next block — and they earn some Bitcoin as a reward."**
* **"This process is called mining. The puzzle is intentionally hard."**
* **"Why does this create trust?"**
  * *"Because to cheat the system, you'd have to out-compute the rest of the network combined."*
  * *"Which is practically impossible and incredibly expensive."*
* **"The downside? Energy."**
  * *"Bitcoin's energy usage is comparable to entire countries."*
  * *"That is a real and fair criticism."*

---

### 🔷 Proof of Stake (Blue Slide Card):
* **"Proof of Stake — this is Ethereum's approach."**
* **"Ethereum recognized the energy problem and made a historic switch in 2022."**
* **"Known as 'The Merge'."**
* **"Instead of competing with computing power, participants lock up — or stake — their own crypto as collateral."**
* **"The network randomly selects validators, weighted by how much they've staked."**
* **"If a validator tries to cheat, they lose their staked funds."**
* **"The economic incentive keeps everyone honest."**
* **"The result? Ethereum cut its energy consumption by ~99.95%."**
* **"That is a fundamental change."**

---

### 🌐 Other Mechanisms to Mention Briefly:
* **"Delegated Proof of Stake:** Lets token holders vote for a smaller group of trusted validators, kind of like electing representatives."
* **"Proof of History:** Used by Solana. It builds timestamps directly into the blockchain for extremely fast transaction processing."

---

### 🖥️ Nodes and Validators:
* **"One more concept to take away: nodes."**
* **"A node is simply a computer that participates in the network."**
* **"It stores a full copy of the blockchain and helps verify transactions."**
* **"Ethereum has tens of thousands of nodes running all over the world."**
* **"Bitcoin has even more."**
* **"This is what decentralization actually looks like in practice."**
* **"Thousands of independent machines keeping each other honest."**
* **"To shut the network down, you'd have to shut all of them down simultaneously."**
* **"That's why these networks are so resilient."**
