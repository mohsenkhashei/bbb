## 🗣️ THE SCRIPT

### 🚀 The New Frontier:
* **"As we look at the immediate future..."**
* **"The most explosive growth isn't happening in finance..."**
* **"It's happening at the intersection of AI and blockchain."**

---

### 🧠 The Existential Crisis of Trust (Verifiable Provenance):
* **"As artificial intelligence becomes incredibly powerful..."**
* **"Society is facing an existential crisis of trust."**
* **"When content can be generated perfectly in seconds, how do we prove what is real?"**
* **"If an AI model outputs a crucial medical decision, how do we audit the training data?"**
* **"Blockchain offers the ultimate antidote to the chaos of AI: verifiable provenance."**
* **"By using cryptographic stamps on a decentralized ledger..."**
* **"We can permanently record where a piece of data came from, who created it..."**
* **"And verify that it hasn’t been tampered with by an AI model."**

---

### 🤖 Economic Rails for Autonomous AI Agents:
* **"But it goes deeper."**
* **"We are moving into a world of autonomous AI agents — software programs that operate independently."**
* **"An AI agent cannot open a traditional bank account."**
* **"It doesn't have a passport or a physical signature."**
* **"But an AI agent can own a crypto wallet."**
* **"It can execute smart contracts."**
* **"Blockchain provides the native economic layer for AI..."**
* **"Allowing autonomous systems to pay for server costs, hire other AI agents..."**
* **"And trade value securely without a human middleman."**

---

### 💡 The Big Takeaway:
> **"AI is the engine of our digital future..."**
> 
> **"But cryptography and blockchain are the guardrails..."**
> 
> **"That will keep it accountable, verifiable, and economically autonomous."**
